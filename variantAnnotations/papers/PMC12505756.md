# Association of CYP3A5 rs15524 and CYP3A7 rs776744 polymorphisms on tac IPV and clinical outcomes in early post-heart transplantation


## Background

The intrapatient variability (IPV) of tacrolimus (Tac) has gradually become a new index for the prognosis of organ transplantation. The gene polymorphism is involved in the pharmacokinetic process of Tac. The aim of this study was to investigate the effects of genetic polymorphisms on Tac IPV and other clinical outcomes in heart transplant recipients during the early post-transplantation period.


## Methods

Our study retrospectively collected the clinical data and genotyping results of 48 heart transplant recipients. SPSS (21.0) and Graphpad Prism (8.0) were used to analyze the effect of gene polymorphism on Tac concentration (C0) and clinical outcome.


## Results

The CYP3A5 rs15524 AA genotype has a higher trough concentrations/dose (C0/D) value than G allele carriers, and CYP3A7 rs776744 CC genotype has a higher C0/D value than T allele carriers. Patients with The CYP3A5 rs15524 and CYP3A7 rs776744 mutants had a higher IPV. One year after heart transplantation, the mortality of wild-type and mutant CYP3A5 rs15524 patients due to rejection was 0 vs. 10.7% (P = 0.255). The mortality caused by rejection in wild-type patients and mutant patients of CYP3A7 rs776744 was 4.5% vs. 7.7% (p = 0.564).


## Conclusions

Genetic polymorphisms of CYP3A5 rs15524 and CYP3A7 rs776744 affect tacrolimus metabolism in heart transplant recipients, significantly affecting Tac C0/D during the early postoperative period. In addition, gene polymorphism may be related to Tac IPV and clinical outcome in patients with early heart transplantation.

## Introduction

Heart transplantation is the ultimate treatment for most patients with end-stage heart failure. Since the first heart transplantation in 1967, survival rates has been greatly improved, and the average survival time of adult heart transplantation has now increased to 11 years. Tac, a macrolide Calcineurin inhibitor (CNI) with potent immunosuppressive properties and a lower incidence of side effects than cyclosporine, has been widely used to prevent post-transplant rejection.

Although Tac is crucial for maintaining immunosuppression during heart transplantation, its narrow therapeutic window and large intra-and inter-individual pharmacokinetic (PK) differences predispose patients to rejection from insufficient drug exposure or toxicity caused by excessive drug accumulation. Using only the instantaneous C0 of Tac at a given time point to assess drug exposure and treatment response has limited utility.

Currently, there is an increasing interest in identifying and validating genetic variations that contribute to IPV. More evidence suggests that high IPV is related to long-term adverse outcomes in kidney transplantation, including the incidence of rejection, graft loss, and patient mortality. Compared with most studies on the correlation between IPV and clinical outcome, there are few studies on the influence of gene polymorphism on IPV. There are many factors affecting IPV, including drug combination, drug compliance, diarrhea and so on. However, the relationship between gene polymorphism and IPV has not been clarified. Currently, little research has investigated the effect of CYP3A5 on IPV in the stable period after renal transplantation, but the results are different. Previous studies focused on patients with stable IPV for at least 3 months after transplantation. However, during the early stage after transplantation, the physiological state of the patient’s intestine, liver, and whole body is still unstable, causing substantial fluctuations in Tac C0, and the relationship between gene polymorphism and Tac IPV has not been clarified.

Polymorphisms in genes encoding drug-metabolizing enzymes and drug transporters are among the most important factors contributing to the altered activity of Tac, leading to Tac IPV generation. Tac is mainly metabolized by the CYP3A enzyme system, among which CYP3A4*1G rs2242480 and CYP3A5*3 rs776746 are the most important isoenzymes. Additionally, the effects of polymorphisms in other drug-metabolizing enzymes (CYP3A5 rs15524, CYP3A7 rs2257401, CYP3A7 rs10211, and CYP3A7 rs776744), signaling factors (STAT3 rs1053004, SUMO4 rs237024, POR rs2868177, MAP3K9 rs8006424, ADRB2 rs1042713, and MAP3K1 rs62355944), regulatory factors (PPARA rs5767743), and cytokines (TLR9 rs352139, IL17A rs2275913, and IL3 rs181781) on Tac metabolism in recipients of cardiac transplant remains unclear.

Additionally, few studies have revealed the PK of Tac during the early stages of heart transplantation. Domestic researchers have shown that maintaining the target therapeutic window of Tac at 15–20 ng/ml within 1 month after heart transplantation is safer and more effective. International guidelines and experts agree that the target concentration of Tac should be 10–15 ng/ml within 3 months post-transplant. Owing to the lack of higher-quality evidence-based medicine, the recommended range in the current guidelines for Tac C0 in the early stage after heart transplantation remained unchanged for nearly 20 years.

Accordingly, this study aimed to retrospectively analyze the distribution of Tac C0 in the early stage after heart transplantation, and systematically explore the effects of gene polymorphisms such as drug metabolizing enzymes, signal transduction factors, cytokines and regulatory factors on Tac pharmacokinetics and Tac IPV. In addition, this paper further discusses the influence of gene polymorphism on long-term clinical outcomes such as acute rejection rate and mortality after transplantation, in order to provide basis for drug management scheme of Tac in Chinese heart transplant recipients.


## Materials and methods


### Study design and population

We reviewed the medical records and laboratory results of patients who recived heart transplantation at our center between March 2021 and February 2024. All patients were induced with Basiliximab 20 mg x2 after operation, and triple-drug immunosuppression (Tac, mycophenolate mofetil, and corticosteroids) was used as immunosuppressant to maintain treatment, and the Tac C0 was monitored regularly. The inclusion criteria were as follows: Chinese recipients of heart transplants, first-time recipients of heart transplants, and no other organ transplant experience. The exclusion criteria were as follows: patients with poor compliance with drug treatment, incomplete dosing regimen and timing of blood collection, patients who are pregnant or lactating, patients undergoing hemodialysis or peritoneal dialysis during the study period, patients who had changed or co-administered other immunosuppressive agents, patients who use CYP3A enzyme inhibitors or inducers, as well as P-gp substrates or inhibitors, such as Wuzhi capsules, voriconazole ect, and patients with impaired liver and renal function. A total of 48 heart transplant recipients were enrolled. All patients had completed genotyping.


### Immunosuppressive regimen

The initial dose of Tac (Hangzhou Zhongmei Huadong Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd., China) is based on the patient’s body Weight and is usually administered as a starting dose of 0.10–0.15 mg/kg/d orally twice daily at 7:00 and 19:00. After that, the dosage was adjusted according to TDM method, and the target C0 was maintained at 10–15 ng/ml in the early stage after heart transplantation. The initial dose of mycophenolate (Shanghai Roche pharmaceutical co., ltd., China) was 0.36–0.72 g, twice a day. The dose of mycophenolate mofetil (Novartis Pharma Stein AG, Switzerland)is generally 0.5–1 g, twice a day and the initial dose of prednisone acetate (Shanghai Xinyi pharmaceutical factory co., ltd., China) is generally 5 mg once a day; the dose was adjusted according to the side effects.


### Data collection

Demographic information and laboratory data of the heart transplant recipients were collected from hospital electronic medical record system. Data recorded included the recipient’s Weight, daily dose, and other laboratory data 1, 2, 3rd week and 1 st month. Because Tac C0 dose not reach steady-state levels within 1 week after heart transplantation, and the patient’s pathophysiology is unstable within 2 weeks, these factors may obscure the impact of genetic factors. In contrast, with extended transplantation time, changes in the glucocorticoid dose can cause changes in the CYP enzyme, affecting the judgment of the results. Therefore, we finally included Tac C0 at 1 month after transplantation.


### Tac IPV

Tac IPV measures the fluctuation of C0 within a patient over a specific time period. The classic calculation formula of Tac IPV is coefficient of variation (CV). CV is a statistical measure of variability, defined as the ratio of standard deviation (SD) to mean, expressed as a percentage. (Calculation formula for CV: CV = SD/Mean*100%).


### Genotyping

Based on the gene loci and potential gene loci affecting the reported Tac PK/PD, we screened 17 single nucleic acid gene loci associated with Tac PK/PD: drug-metabolizing enzymes (CYP3A5*3 rs776746, CYP3A4*1G rs2242480, CYP3A5 rs15524, CYP3A7 rs2257401, CYP3A7 rs10211, and CYP3A7 rs776744), signaling factors (STAT3 rs1053004, SUMO4 rs237024, POR rs2868177, MAP3K9 rs8006424, ADRB2 rs1042713, and MAP3K1 rs62355944), regulatory factors (POR rs2868177, PPARA rs5767743), and cytokines (TLR9 rs352139, IL17A rs2275913, and IL3 rs181781). Genomic DNA was separated from the peripheral leukocytes of blood samples using kits (Beijing tiangen biochemical technology co., ltd, China). Genotype analysis of selected single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) was performed by direct sequencing. Polymerase chain reaction (PCR) primers are listed in Supplementary Table 1.


### Serum creatinine

The serum creatinine value of each patient at 1 month after heart transplantation was collected to evaluate the early nephrotoxicity of Tac.


### Rejection

Endomyocardial biopsy (EMB) was performed regularly at 1 month, 3 months, 6 months and 12 months after heart transplantation. Evaluation of biopsy results was made according to the diagnostic criteria of acute rejection established by the International Society for Heart and Lung Transplantation (ISHLT). Heart transplant recipients were followed up for one year, and the mortality caused by acute rejection was counted.


### Statistical analysis

The distribution of continuous data was analyzed by the Kolmogorov–Smirnov test. Results that conform to normal distribution are expressed by the average standard deviation, and differences between groups are compared by single sample t-test or one-way analysis of variance (ANOVA). Mann-Whitney U test was used to compare the numerical variables of non-normal distribution results. The difference for P value of 0.05 or less is considered to be significant. The statistical analyses was conducted with SPSS (version 21.0) and GraphPad Prism (version 8.0).

## Discussion

As a first-line anti-rejection drug in heart transplantation, Tac has a narrow therapeutic window, which is greatly affected by drug combination and gene polymorphisms, resulting in considerable variability among individuals. The 30-day postoperative period is critical for the early recovery of heart transplant recipients, during which they experience high intra-and inter-individual variability in Tac owing to the presence of infections and other high-risk factors, posing a great challenge in rationalizing the use of Tac. In our study, we retrospectively analyzed post-transplantation Tac C0 in 87 heart transplant recipients to identify the influence of gene polymorphism on the Tac IPV and other clinical outcomes.

Our results shown that the effective therapeutic C0 of Tac in the early stage after heart transplantation was only 34% in the range of 10–15 ng/ml, and the average C0 of Tac in the early stage after heart transplantation was lower than the target C0 range. 1However, whether the target C0 of Tac can be reduced or not needs to be studied in a large sample in the future. As an indicator of Tac pharmacokinetics, C0/D ratio can reflect the comprehensive effect of all factors affecting Tac metabolism. A significant difference was observed in C0/D among the patients at different times postoperatively, suggesting that there was a difference in the ability to metabolize Tac at different times.

Routine measurement of Tac C0 in whole blood in clinical work is used to monitor its therapeutic effect and adverse reactions. TDM results can exhibit delay, making it challenging to achieve an initial dosing regimen in some patients who are fast-metabolizers, resulting in low and fluctuating C0 values in the early patients. This suggests that the initial dose should be adjusted based on the patient’s metabolic ability. CYP3A5 and CYP3A4 are the main enzymes involved in Tac metabolism. Among these, CYP3A5*3 rs776746 has a high mutation frequency in the population, which significantly correlated with Tac elimination. Consequently, this genotype has been recognized as a biomarker of the Tac metabolic phenotype in most studies. Studies have shown that CYP3A5 gene polymorphism is associated with the PK of Tac, and other studies have suggested that the CYP3A5*3 rs776746 gene polymorphism may not correlate with Tac C0 within 1 month after heart transplantation. Our results showed that CYP3A5 rs15524 and CYP3A7 rs776744 genotypes significantly correlated with C0/D values in heart transplant recipients. Specifically, the CYP3A5 rs15524 AA type had higher C0/D values than the G allele carriers, further validating that the CYP3A5 gene polymorphism is associated with Tac PK. In stable renal transplant recipients, CYP3A5 rs15524 significantly influenced C0/D values of Tac and sirolimus at different times from 4 to 24 months after transplantation. CYP3A5 rs15524 is also involved in the metabolism of sirolimus in the early stage after renal transplantation. Studies have shown that the C0/D of sirolimus in CYP3A5 rs15524 AA genotype carriers is higher than that in patients with AG and GG alleles on the 7th day, 1 st month and 3rd month after renal transplantation. Our results are consistent with previous studies, CYP3A5 rs15524 AA genotype carriers had higher Tac C0/D values. CYP3A5 rs15524 involves an A > G base substitution, where the wild-type allele is A and the mutant allele is G. Situated in the 3’ untranslated region (3’UTR), this SNP may impact mRNA stability, thereby altering enzyme expression and activity. Research indicates that rs15524 could regulate CYP3A5 expression by influencing the targeting of hsa-miR-500a-5p to CYP3A5. All the above results indicate that CYP3A5 gene polymorphism plays an important role in Tac metabolism after different organ transplantation, and it should be combined with multiple genes to adjust its dose in clinic.

CYP3A7 was initially thought to be expressed only during fetal development; however, recent studies have detected high expression of CYP3A7 in more than half of adult livers, contributing approximately 20% of the total CYP3A. Sun et al. found that CYP3A7 rs2257401 and rs10211 were involved in cyclosporine metabolism at different times after kidney transplantation in China. A whole-exome sequencing study showed that CYP3A7 rs2257401 was associated with Tac C0/D in adult recipients of kidney transplants. These findings suggest that CYP3A7 polymorphisms may serve as important genetic markers of Tac PK in transplant patients. Studies have revealed that CYP3A7 rs776744 is associated with increased mRNA and protein expression levels of CYP3A5, which accounts for individual differences in CYP3A5 beyond the known variations. The wild-type allele of CYP3A7 rs776744 is C, while the mutant allele is T. This C→T mutation is classified as a gain-of-function variant, and individuals carrying the rs776744 T allele exhibit significantly elevated CYP3A5 mRNA expression levels. Similarly, our study found that CYP3A7 rs776744 polymorphisms were significantly correlated with TAC C0/D, with CYP3A7 rs776744 C gene carriers having significantly higher C0/D than non-carriers.

At present, Tac IPV has become a new marker to evaluate the risk of adverse outcome of solid organ transplantation. Tac IPV measures the fluctuation of C0 in patients in a specific time period, and uses various indicators to quantify it, such as SD and CV. Due to CV could standardize variability across groups with differing mean levels, alignment with established methods in the field. In this study, we used the classic CV calculation method to represent Tac IPV and investigate the influence of gene polymorphisms. Our study demonstrated the statistically significant effects of CYP3A5 rs15524 and CYP3A7 rs776744 SNPs on Tac IPV. Patients with CYP3A5 rs15524 GG or CYP3A7 rs776744 TT genotype showed higher Tac IPV than patients without genotype. CYP3A5*3 genotype has been widely studied in pharmacogenomics and individual variability of Tac C0, but its influence on Tac IPV remains controversial. Korean researchers found that Tac IPV in CYP3A5 *3/*3 genotype was over 50% higher than in CYP3A5 *1/*1 and *1/3 groups. However, other studies showed no such relationship between CYP3A5 genotype and Tac IPV. In our study, Tac IPV in CYP3A5 *3/*3 genotype patients was higher than in *1/*1 and *1/3 carriers, with no significant difference. Due to variations in IPV calculation methods, transplant time and graft status, the influence of CYP3A5*3 polymorphism on Tac IPV after heart transplantation needs further clarification. In addition, patients with poor compliance were excluded from our study, which means that our study has some limitations. Poor compliance of patients is one of the important factors affecting the Tac IPV and clinical outcome. It will be an important direction to study the relationship between compliance and CYP3A enzyme gene polymorphism in the future.

EMB is considered as an internationally recognized standard for the diagnosis of acute rejection after heart transplantation. At present, the frequency of monitoring acute rejection by endocardial myocardial biopsy is different in different heart transplant centers. EMB monitoring is regularly performed in our hospital one month, three months, six months and 12 months after the immune induction treatment of heart transplant recipients. In this study, EMB results of 48 heart transplant recipients within one year after operation showed that the incidence of acute rejection and mortality caused by rejection in CYP3A5 rs15524 and CYP3A7 rs776744 mutant patients were higher than those in wild-type patients, but there was no statistical significance. Notably, the C0/D values in patients with acute rejection or those who died were lowerer than in those without acute rejection or death (Supplementary material Figure S1). This phenomenon may be attributed to the fact that the physiological state of patients with acute rejection or those facing death is likely to undergo changes, thereby impacting the metabolism of tacrolimus. In addition, the abnormal activation of the immune system may interfere with the normal drug metabolism pathway or change the binding rate between drugs and plasma proteins, thus accelerating the metabolism of tacrolimus, resulting in the decrease of trough concentration and C0/D value at the same dose. However, due to the limitation of the sample size, no statistically significant difference was observed.

In previous studies, the influence of CYP3A5 gene polymorphism on the incidence of acute rejection is still controversial. Many studies on renal transplant recipients have indicated that CYP3A5 gene polymorphism has no effect on the incidence of acute rejection. However, a meta-analysis showed that the occurrence rate of acute rejection of CYP3A5 non-expression recipients within one month after organ transplantation (kidney and liver) was significantly lower than that of CYP3A5 expression recipients. These studies are limited to liver and kidney transplantation, and the influence of gene polymorphism on the prognosis of heart transplantation still needs further discussion.

The results of this study confirmed that single nucleotide polymorphisms of CYP3A5 rs15524 and CYP3A7 rs776744 were significantly correlated with Tac C0/D after heart transplantation, and CYP3A enzyme gene polymorphism may be the reason for the difference in bioavailability of Tac among individuals. In addition, one year after heart transplantation, the mortality and acute rejection rate of CYP3A5 rs 15,524 and CYP3A7 rs 776,744 mutant patients were higher than those of wild type. But this difference is not statistical, which may be related to the lack of sample size. Because the follow-up time of patients in this study is generally short, the research results are limited to the early stage after heart transplantation, and large-sample multicenter research is needed for further verification in the future.


## Conclusions

In conclusion, we demonstrate for the first time differences in C0/D ratios of Tac in recipients after heart transplantation associated with the polymorphisms CYP3A5 rs15524 and CYP3A7 rs776744, which explains the individual differences of some patients. The IPV of Tac in patients with CYP3A5 rs15524 and CYP3A7 rs776744 mutations was higher, and the incidence and mortality of acute rejection was higher.
